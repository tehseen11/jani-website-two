About Kandha Security

A Homegrown Odisha Agency, Built on Discipline

Founded in Dhenkanal in 2011, Kandha Security Services Limited has grown from a single-site guarding contract into one of Odisha's most trusted physical security providers — serving industrial plants, corporate campuses, hospitals, schools and residential communities across the state.

Every guard is verified, uniformed, trained at our in-house academy and supervised through a structured duty-roster system, giving clients accountable, audit-ready security operations rather than ad-hoc manpower.
