Who We Are

Odisha's dependable name in manned guarding & electronic security.

Headquartered in Dhenkanal, Kandha Security Services Limited was founded to bring disciplined, ex-servicemen-led protection to the industries, institutions and homes of Odisha. What began as a single-site guarding contract has grown into a full-spectrum security company serving industrial belts, corporate parks, hospitals, campuses and residential communities across the state.

Every guard is background-verified, uniformed and trained at our in-house facility before deployment, and every site is backed by a 24/7 control room and supervisory patrol network.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Kandha Security Services PSARA licensed?+

Yes. We operate under a valid Private Security Agencies Regulation Act (PSARA) license issued for the state of Odisha, and all deployments comply with applicable labour law.

What is the minimum contract duration?+

Standard manned-guarding contracts run for a minimum of 3 months, with monthly renewal thereafter. Short-term coverage is available for events and construction sites.

Do you provide armed security personnel?+

We provide unarmed guards, supervisors and quick-reaction teams. Where a client's risk profile requires armed protocols, we coordinate with licensed government agencies as permitted by law.

How quickly can guards be deployed?+

Standard deployment is within 48–72 hours of contract signing. Emergency or event-based deployment can often be arranged within 24 hours, subject to manpower availability.

Which areas of Odisha do you cover?+

We are headquartered in Dhenkanal with active sites in Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Angul, Jajpur and Talcher, and can assess coverage for other districts on request.

Need security personnel on short notice?

Our rapid-deployment team can have verified guards on-site within 24–72 hours anywhere in Odisha.
