Our Story

Founded Locally, Trusted Statewide

Kandha Security Services Limited was founded in Dhenkanal in 2011 by a team of former armed-forces personnel who saw a gap in professionally managed, accountable manned guarding in the region.

What began as a 15-guard operation protecting a single rice-processing facility has grown into a full-spectrum security company with an in-house training academy, a 24x7 control room, and mobile patrol units covering five districts of Odisha.

Today, we support manufacturing plants, hospitals, schools, corporate campuses and residential communities — always guided by the same principle we started with: security is a discipline, not a formality.

Our Mission

To deliver disciplined, verifiable and responsive security services that protect people, property and operations across Odisha — with the same accountability our clients would demand of their own teams.

Our Vision

To be Odisha's most trusted homegrown security partner — recognised for professionalism, transparent compliance, and the standard of care we set for the guarding profession in the state.
