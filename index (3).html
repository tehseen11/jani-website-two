Who We Are

A disciplined force built on training, integrity and accountability.

Headquartered in Dhenkanal, Odisha, Kandha Security Services Limited is a PSARA-licensed security agency providing manned guarding, electronic surveillance and rapid-response services to industrial plants, corporate campuses, hospitals, educational institutions and residential communities across the state.

Every guard we deploy completes structured training in access control, fire safety, first aid and conflict de-escalation before stepping onto a client site — because a security company is only as strong as its weakest post.
